Accounts — ★ Bank Details Here

/accounts
Where to configure bank account details in the UI: Navigate to Accounts in the sidebar (under Finance) → click "Add Account" in the top-right corner → scroll to the bottom of the form. You will find Institution Name (e.g. "Chase Bank") and Last 4 Digits (e.g. "4821"). These appear on the account card as: Bank · Chase Bank ···· 4821.
Step-by-step — Adding a bank account with details
1
Click Accounts in the left sidebar (Finance section)
2
Click the "Add Account" button (top-right, blue, with + icon)
3
Fill in Name (e.g. "Main Checking") and set Type to BANK
4
Enter Initial Balance to match your actual bank balance today
5
(New) Enter Institution Name: the name of your bank (e.g. "Chase Bank", "HSBC")
6
(New) Enter Last 4 Digits: the last 4 digits of your account or card number (e.g. "4821"). Exactly 4 digits required.
7
Click Create. The card will show: Bank · Chase Bank ···· 4821

Scheduled Jobs

JobClassScheduleAction
Recurring Processor RecurringScheduler Every hour (@Scheduled) Finds all active RecurringTransaction rules where nextDue ≤ now. Creates a transaction for each, advances nextDue by frequency, sends notification.
Insight Generator InsightService Monthly (1st of month) Runs 11 insight rules for all users. Creates Insight records. Sends notification if new insights generated.
Weekly Summary NotificationService Weekly (Monday) For users with notifyWeeklySummary=true: creates a summary notification with weekly income/expense totals.
